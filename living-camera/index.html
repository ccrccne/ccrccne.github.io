Living Camera

La cámara se balancea al caminar, se inclina al moverte de lado o girar, tiene peso al mirar y rebota suave al aterrizar.

The camera sways as you walk, leans when you strafe or turn, has weight when you look around and bounces softly when you land.

  • Mixin
  • 1.20.1 – 26.3
  • Fabric
  • Forge

Modrinth ↗ CurseForge ↗ GitHub ↗

Un mod que le da vida a la cámara en primera persona. Cada tick calcula cuánto debe inclinarse y balancearse según cómo te mueves, y al dibujar suaviza esos valores para que se vea fluido a cualquier FPS. El movimiento se nota sin marear, y cada efecto se puede ajustar o apagar.

A mod that brings the first-person camera to life. Every tick it works out how much the view should lean and sway based on how you move, and it smooths those values while drawing so it looks fluid at any FPS. You feel the movement without getting dizzy, and every effect can be adjusted or turned off.
